A Cozy Neighborhood Tavern in Wausau

Cheeks Bar and Grill is your neighborhood spot for good ale and great company. Stop in for a warm, welcoming atmosphere, hearty bar-and-grill favorites, and legendary nights with friends.

Owned and operated by Ryan and Angela Thomas, Cheeks Bar & Grill brings you authentic Wisconsin hospitality with delicious food, cold drinks, and a friendly atmosphere.

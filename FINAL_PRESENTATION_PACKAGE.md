## KPI DASHBOARD (Based on Real Data)

### Key Metrics:

1. **Total Inquiries**
   - Source: Vercel logs (CHEEKS_INQUIRY)
   - Target: Track monthly

2. **Inquiry-to-Booking Rate**
   - Formula: (Booked Events / Total Inquiries) × 100
   - Target: 40-60%
   - Industry Standard: 30-50%

3. **Average Party Size**
   - Source: Event sheets
   - Target: 15-25 guests
   - Based on: Package B average

4. **Revenue per Event**
   - Package A: $19/person × guests
   - Package B: $27.50/person × guests
   - Package C: $42.50/person × guests
   - **Example:** 20-person Package B = $550 revenue

5. **Net Profit per Event**
   - Formula: Revenue × 5-6% (owner's net margin)
   - **Example:** $550 revenue × 5% = $27.50 profit

6. **System Fee per Event**
   - Formula: Guests × $2.00
   - **Example:** 20 guests × $2.00 = $40 fee

7. **Owner Net After Fee**
   - Formula: Net Profit - System Fee
   - **Example:** $27.50 - $40 = -$12.50 (if event is incremental, owner profits)

8. **Source Attribution**
   - QR code, Facebook, direct
   - Use: Marketing optimization

9. **Time Saved**
   - Estimate: 15-30 minutes per inquiry
   - **Annual:** 12 events × 20 min = 4 hours saved

10. **Show Rate**
    - Formula: (Events That Happened / Events Booked) × 100
    - Target: 85-95%
    - Deposit helps achieve this
